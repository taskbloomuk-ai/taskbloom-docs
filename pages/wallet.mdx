## Requesting a Payout

### Step-by-Step

1. Go to **`/payout`**
2. Enter the amount you want to withdraw

> **Minimum:** $10.00

3. Select your **payment method**:
   - **PayPal** — Fast and widely available
   - **Stripe** — Credit card payouts
   - **Wise** — International bank transfers
   - **Crypto** — Cryptocurrency

4. Enter your **payment email or address**
5. Review the **fee** and **net amount**
6. Click **Submit**

### Fees & Timing

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Fee** | 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction |
| **Processing** | 72-hour hold period |
| **Minimum withdrawal** | $10.00 |

## Transaction History

**URL:** `/transactions`

Every financial movement on TaskBloom is recorded:

| Transaction Type | Description | Effect on Balance |
|-----------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **task_earning** | Payment for approved task | + Credit |
| **referral_commission** | Commission from referral's task | + Credit |
| **payout_request** | Withdrawal you requested | - Debit |
| **payout_completed** | Withdrawal processed | - Debit |
| **bonus** | Platform bonuses or rewards | + Credit |

You can filter transactions by type to quickly find specific entries.

## Understanding Your Earning Cap

Each package has a **monthly earning cap**. This is the maximum amount you can earn from tasks in a single month.

| Package | Price | Earning Cap | How It Works |
|---------|-------|-------------|--------------|
| **Bronze** | $9.99 | $11.99/month | Cap = 120% of package price |
| **Silver** | $49.99 | $74.99/month | Cap = 150% of package price |
| **Gold** | $99.99 | $199.98/month | Cap = 200% of package price |
| **Platinum** | $499.99 | $1,499.97/month | Cap = 300% of package price |
| **Diamond** | $999.99 | **Unlimited** | No cap |

> **Example:** As a Bronze member paying $9.99/month, you can earn up to $11.99 from tasks this month. Once you hit this limit, new tasks won't be available until the 1st of next month — or until you upgrade.

## Common Questions

> **Q: When are referral commissions paid?**
> **A:** Commissions are credited when your referral's task is **approved** by the advertiser.

> **Q: Can I withdraw if my balance is below $10?**
> **A:** No, the minimum payout is $10.00. Keep completing tasks until you reach this threshold.

> **Q: How do I track my earnings over time?**
> **A:** Visit `/wallet` to see your 14-day earnings chart, weekly stats, and monthly totals.

> **Q: What happens to my earnings if I cancel my account?**
> **A:** Contact support to process your remaining balance before account closure.
