# Reseller Wallet & Transactions

Manage your reseller wallet for voucher purchases and withdrawals.

---

## Wallet Overview

**URL:** `/reseller/wallet`

Your reseller wallet tracks:
- **Available Balance** - Funds available for purchases
- **Pending Deposits** - Recent deposits awaiting confirmation
- **Total Spent** - All-time spending on vouchers
- **Withdrawal History** - Past withdrawals

---

## Depositing Funds

1. Go to `/reseller/wallet`
2. Click **"Deposit Funds"**
3. Enter the amount
4. Select payment method
5. Complete the transaction

---

## Withdrawing Funds

1. Navigate to `/reseller/wallet`
2. Click **"Withdraw"**
3. Enter amount (minimum withdrawal applies)
4. Provide payment details
5. Submit request

> **Processing time:** Withdrawals typically process within 72 hours.

---

## Transaction Ledger

**URL:** `/reseller/ledger`

View a complete history of all financial transactions:
- **Deposits** - Funds added to wallet
- **Purchases** - Voucher costs deducted
- **Commissions** - Earnings credited
- **Withdrawals** - Funds removed

---

## Balance Management Tips

- Keep sufficient balance for anticipated orders
- Withdraw profits regularly
- Monitor the ledger for all transactions
- Set up alerts for low balance warnings
