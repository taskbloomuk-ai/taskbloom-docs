# Voucher Inventory

Manage your voucher stock and track usage.

---

## Understanding Inventory

Your inventory shows all voucher codes you've purchased, organized by:
- **Status** - Active, sold, expired, revoked
- **Batch** - Group purchases
- **Value** - Registration vs. upgrade vouchers
- **Usage** - How many have been redeemed

---

## Viewing Inventory

1. Navigate to `/reseller/inventory`
2. View all vouchers with status indicators
3. Filter by status, batch, or date range
4. Export inventory data for reporting

### Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Active** | Available for sale, not yet used |
| **Sold** | Redeemed by a worker |
| **Expired** | Past validity date |
| **Revoked** | Admin-disabled vouchers |

---

## Managing Stock

- **Low stock alerts** - Dashboard shows when inventory runs low
- **Repurchase** - Quick reorder from past batches
- **Bulk operations** - Activate/deactivate multiple vouchers at once

---

## Best Practices

- Maintain 2-3 weeks of inventory to avoid stockouts
- Monitor which voucher types sell fastest
- Reorder before inventory drops below 20%
