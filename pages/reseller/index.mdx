# Reseller Guide

Welcome to the TaskBloom Reseller Program. As a reseller, you purchase voucher codes in bulk at wholesale prices and distribute them to workers — earning profit on each sale.

---

## What You Can Do

- **Purchase voucher batches** at discounted wholesale prices
- **Manage inventory** of active, sold, and expired vouchers
- **Sell vouchers** to workers who need them for registration or package upgrades
- **Track sales** and commissions through your dashboard
- **Manage wallet** — deposits, purchases, and withdrawals

---

## Why Become a Reseller?

Vouchers are essential for the TaskBloom ecosystem — every new worker needs a valid voucher to register. As a reseller, you:

- Buy vouchers in bulk at lower per-unit prices
- Sell them at market rates to earn profit margins
- Build a recurring customer base as new workers need vouchers
- Access detailed sales analytics to optimize pricing

---

## Getting Started

1. **Register** at `/reseller/register` (may require admin approval)
2. **Fund your wallet** via deposit
3. **Purchase your first voucher batch**
4. **Distribute vouchers** to workers through your channels

## Reseller Dashboard

**URL:** `/reseller/dashboard`

Your dashboard gives you a complete overview with these widgets:

| Widget | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Active Vouchers** | Currently available voucher count |
| **Total Sales** | All-time sales revenue |
| **Wallet Balance** | Available funds for purchases |
| **Pending Orders** | Orders being processed |
