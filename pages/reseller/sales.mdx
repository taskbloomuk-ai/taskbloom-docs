# Sales & Commissions

Track your reseller sales performance and earnings.

---

## Sales Dashboard

**URL:** `/reseller/sales`

View comprehensive sales analytics:
- **Total Vouchers Sold** - All-time sales volume
- **Revenue** - Total sales value
- **Profit** - Revenue minus purchase costs
- **Daily/Weekly/Monthly Trends** - Sales patterns

---

## Commissions

**URL:** `/reseller/commissions`

As a reseller, you earn commissions on:
- **Direct Sales** - Vouchers you sell to workers
- **Referral Sales** - Sales from referrals you bring in

Commission rates are displayed on your commissions page.

---

## Sales Reports

Generate detailed reports for:
- **Daily sales summary** - Quick overview
- **Monthly performance** - Long-term trends
- **Customer analytics** - Repeat buyers, new customers

Reports can be viewed online or exported for your records.

---

## Tips for Maximizing Sales

1. **Build relationships** with worker communities who need vouchers
2. **Offer bundle deals** — registration + upgrade voucher packs
3. **Monitor peak times** — more workers register on weekends
4. **Keep inventory stocked** — never run out of popular voucher types
5. **Track competitor pricing** — stay competitive in the market
