# Orders & Purchases

Learn how to purchase voucher batches and track your orders.

---

## Purchasing Vouchers

### Step 1: Navigate to Purchase

Go to `/reseller/orders/purchase`

### Step 2: Select Voucher Type

- **Registration Vouchers** - For new worker signups
- **Upgrade Vouchers** - For package upgrades

### Step 3: Choose Quantity

Select how many vouchers you want to purchase. Bulk pricing applies:
- 10-50 vouchers: Standard wholesale price
- 51-200 vouchers: Volume discount
- 201+ vouchers: Enterprise pricing

### Step 4: Confirm Purchase

Review the total cost and confirm. Funds are deducted from your reseller wallet immediately.

---

## Order History

**URL:** `/reseller/orders`

View all your past purchases:
- **Order ID** - Unique identifier
- **Voucher Type** - Registration or upgrade
- **Quantity** - Number of vouchers purchased
- **Total Cost** - Amount paid
- **Status** - Completed, processing, failed
- **Date** - Purchase timestamp

---

## Order Statuses

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Completed** | Vouchers added to inventory |
| **Processing** | Awaiting fulfillment |
| **Failed** | Payment issue, contact support |

---

## Troubleshooting

> **Issue:** "Insufficient wallet balance"
> **Solution:** Deposit funds at `/reseller/wallet`

> **Issue:** "Order failed to process"
> **Solution:** Contact support with the order details
