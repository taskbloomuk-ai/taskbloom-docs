# Worker Getting Started

Welcome to TaskBloom as a task worker! This guide will help you start earning money by completing simple online tasks.

---

## What You Can Do

As a worker on TaskBloom, you can:
- **Find available tasks** - Browse and claim task assignments
- **Complete tasks** - Follow instructions and submit proof
- **Earn money** - Get paid for approved submissions
- **Withdraw earnings** - Transfer money to your account
- **Refer friends** - Earn commissions from their activity
- **Upgrade your package** - Unlock more tasks and benefits

---

## Quick Start Checklist

> **Step 1:** Register at `/register` (requires a voucher code)
> **Step 2:** Go to dashboard at `/tasks`
> **Step 3:** Click "Get Task" to find available work
> **Step 4:** Complete the task following instructions
> **Step 5:** Submit proof (screenshot/link/none as required)
> **Step 6:** Wait for approval - typically 24-72 hours
> **Step 7:** Earnings added to wallet upon approval

## Task Limits & Rules

- **Daily limit** - Resets at midnight
- **Monthly limit** - Resets on the 1st of each month
- **Earning cap** - Monthly maximum based on package
- **Concurrent tasks** - Can only have 1-10 active at a time
- **24-hour cooldown** - Can't repeat same task type for 24 hours
- **48-hour deadline** - Must submit before task expires

---

## Getting & Starting a Task

1. Go to `/tasks` or click **"Get Task"**
2. If available, you'll be redirected to the task page
3. Read the instructions carefully
4. Click **"Start Task"** to begin
5. Complete the required action
6. Submit proof when done

> **Warning:** Tasks have a **48-hour deadline**. If you don't submit within 48 hours, the task expires and you earn nothing.

---

## Task Blockers

| Reason | Message | Solution |
|--------|---------|----------|
| No package | "No active package" | Purchase package at /packages |
| Daily limit reached | "Daily task limit reached" | Wait until midnight |
| Monthly limit reached | "Monthly task limit reached" | Wait until 1st of month |
| Earning cap hit | "Monthly earning limit reached" | Wait until 1st of month or upgrade |
| Task in progress | "You have a task in progress" | Complete current task |
| No tasks | "No tasks available" | Check back later |

## Proof Submission

### Screenshot Tasks
Take a clear screenshot showing you completed the action.
- **Google Review** - Show the published review
- **Instagram Follow** - Show you're following the account
- **YouTube Subscribe** - Show the subscribed button

### Link Tasks
Provide a URL verifying completion.
- **Survey** - Confirmation page URL

### No Proof Tasks
Simply complete the action - no proof needed.
- **Facebook Video View** - Watch the video
- **TikTok View** - Watch the video
