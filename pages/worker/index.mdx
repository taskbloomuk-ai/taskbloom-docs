# Worker Guide

Welcome to TaskBloom! This guide covers everything you need to know about completing tasks and earning money on our platform.

---

## How TaskBloom Works

TaskBloom connects you with advertisers who need real people to complete simple online actions. You get paid for each task you complete successfully.

**The earning cycle is simple:**

1. **Get a Task** — Click "Get Task" on your dashboard
2. **Complete It** — Follow the instructions (watch, follow, review, etc.)
3. **Submit Proof** — Upload a screenshot or link showing completion
4. **Get Paid** — Earnings are added to your wallet upon approval

## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. Go to `/register`
2. Enter your **email address**
3. Create a **password** (at least 8 characters)
4. Enter your **full name**
5. Enter a **voucher code** (required — get one from an advertiser or reseller)
6. Enter a **referral code** (optional — if someone invited you)
7. Select your **country**
8. Click **Create Account**

> **Important:** A valid voucher code is required to register. Vouchers are available from advertisers and resellers. Without one, registration cannot be completed.

### Your Dashboard

**URL:** `/tasks`

Your dashboard is your command center. Here's what you'll see:

| Element | What It Shows |
|---------|---------------|
| **Tasks Completed** | Your all-time approved task count |
| **Today's Earnings** | Money earned today |
| **Active Tasks** | Tasks you're currently working on |
| **Pending Approval** | Submitted tasks awaiting review |
| **Daily Limit** | Tasks remaining today (resets at midnight) |
| **Monthly Limit** | Tasks remaining this month (resets on the 1st) |
| **Earnings Progress** | How close you are to your monthly earning cap |

## Finding & Completing Tasks

### Getting a Task

1. Go to your dashboard at `/tasks`
2. Click **"Get Task"** (or visit `/tasks/available`)
3. If a task is available, you'll be taken to the task page
4. Read the instructions carefully
5. Click **"Start Task"** to begin

### While Working

- You have **48 hours** to complete and submit the task
- The task shows you exactly what action to take
- Some tasks include a **target URL** — visit the link provided

### Submitting Your Work

1. Complete the required action
2. Click **"Submit"** on the task page
3. Provide proof based on task type:
   - **Screenshot**: Upload a clear image showing completion
   - **Link**: Paste the URL verifying completion
   - **None**: No proof required — just confirm completion

> **Tip:** Take clear, well-lit screenshots that show the completed action. Blurry or incomplete screenshots may be rejected.

### What Happens Next

- **Submitted** — Your task is sent for review
- **Approved** — Earnings are credited to your wallet
- **Rejected** — You'll see the reason; you can try another task after 24 hours

Review typically takes **24-72 hours**. Check your dashboard for status updates.

| You See This | Meaning | How to Fix |
|-------------|---------|------------|
| "No active package" | Your package expired or you don't have one | Purchase or renew at `/packages` |
| "Daily task limit reached" | Used all today's tasks | Wait until midnight |

| "Task in progress" | You have an active task | Complete or cancel it first |
| "No tasks available" | No campaigns in your region | Check back later |

## Managing Your Tasks

### Viewing Task History

**URL:** `/tasks/history`

See all your past tasks with:
- **Status** — Approved, rejected, submitted, expired
- **Earnings** — Amount earned for approved tasks
- **Date** — When you completed each task

### Canceling a Task

If you're unable to complete a task:
1. Go to the task page
2. Click **"Cancel Task"**
3. The task returns to available pool

> **Note:** You can only cancel tasks that are in "locked" or "in progress" status. Already submitted tasks cannot be canceled.

## Earning More

### Upgrade Your Package

Higher packages give you more tasks and higher earning caps:
- Upgrade at `/packages/upgrade`
- Use a **voucher code** for the target package, or meet **achievement requirements**

### Refer Friends

Every person you refer who joins and completes tasks earns you commission:
- **Level 1** (direct referrals): 5% of their earnings
- **Level 2** (their referrals): 3%
- **Level 3**: 1%
- Plus **bonus daily tasks** for each active referral

Find your referral code at `/referrals` and share your link: `taskbloom.co.uk/register?ref=YOURCODE`

## Wallet & Payouts

**URL:** `/wallet`

### Your Balance

- **Wallet Balance** — Available to withdraw
- **Task Earnings** — Money from completed task
- **Referral Earnings** — Commissions from your referrals
- **Total Earned** — Everything you've earned all-time
- **Pending Payouts** — Withdrawals being processed

### Requesting a Payout

1. Go to `/payout`
2. Enter the amount (minimum **$10.00**)
3. Choose your payment method: **PayPal, Stripe, Wise, or Crypto**
4. Enter your payment email or address
5. Submit the request

### Fees & Timing

- **Fee:** 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
- **Processing:** 72-hour hold period
- **Status:** You'll see "pending" until it's processed

## Tips for Success

1. **Complete tasks promptly** — You have 48 hours, but faster completion means more time for new tasks
2. **Take clear screenshots** — Clear proof gets approved faster
3. **Build your referral network** — Each referral adds bonus tasks and commissions
4. **Upgrade at the right time** — When you consistently hit your daily limit, it's time to upgrade
5. **Check daily** — New tasks appear throughout the day
6. **Diversify task types** — Don't rely on just one type of task
