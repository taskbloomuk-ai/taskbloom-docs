# Advertiser Guide

Welcome to TaskBloom for advertisers. This guide covers everything you need to launch successful engagement campaigns.

---

## What Is TaskBloom for Advertisers?

TaskBloom lets you create **engagement campaigns** where real people complete specific actions for your brand. Think of it as a marketplace where you offer micro-tasks, and workers choose to complete them for payment.

**Common campaign goals:**
- Increase social media followers and engagement
- Generate Google reviews and ratings
- Drive video views and watch time
- Boost app installations
- Collect survey responses

## Getting Started

### Creating an Advertiser Account

1. Go to `/advertiser/register`
2. Enter your business email
3. Create a password (8+ characters)
4. Complete your company profile

### Your Dashboard

**URL:** `/advertiser/dashboard`

| Widget | What It Shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Active Campaigns** | Your currently running campaigns |
| **Total Spent** | How much you've spent across all campaigns |
| **Tasks Today** | Submissions received today |
| **Wallet Balance** | Available funds for campaigns |

## Creating a Campaign

### Step 1: New Campaign

Navigate to `/advertiser/campaign/new`

### Step 2: Select Task Type

Choose the action you want workers to complete:

| Task Type | Best For | Proof Required |
|-----------|----------|----------------|
| **Google Review** | Building trust through authentic reviews | Screenshot |
| **Facebook Video View** | Content promotion & reach | None |
| **Instagram Follow** | Growing account following | Screenshot |
| **Instagram Like** | Boosting post engagement | Screenshot |
| **Survey Completion** | Market research & feedback | Link |
| **TikTok View** | Viral content promotion | None |
| **TikTok Like** | Video engagement growth | Screenshot |
| **YouTube Subscribe** | Channel subscriber growth | Screenshot |
| **YouTube Like** | Video engagement | Screenshot |
| **App Download** | App install campaigns | Screenshot |

### Step 3: Configure Your Campaign

| Setting | What to Enter | Tips |
|---------|---------------|------|
| **Campaign Name** | Internal name for your campaign | Be descriptive |
| **Description** | Instructions for workers | Be clear and specific |
| **Target URL** | Where workers go | Double-check the link works |
| **Custom Instructions** | Additional guidance | Include account/page to engage with |
| **Budget** | Maximum total spend | Start small, scale up |
| **Cost per Task** | Payout per completion | Higher payouts attract more workers |
| **Daily Cap** | Max completions per day | Prevents budget from exhausting too fast |

### Step 4: Targeting

Reach the right audience:

| Targeting Option | What It Does |
|-----------------|--------------|
| **Countries** | Limit to specific countries (US, UK, AU, etc.) |
| **Languages** | Filter by preferred language |
| **Min Account Age** | Require accounts older than X days |

> **Tip:** Broad targeting gets results faster. Narrow targeting is more precise but may take longer to complete.

### Step 5: Launch

Review your campaign settings and click **Create Campaign**. Your campaign will start in **Draft** status. Review it one more time, then **Launch** to make it active.

## Campaign Lifecycle

### Statuses

| Status | Meaning | What You Can Do |
|--------|---------|-----------------|
| **Draft** | Saved but not running | Edit or launch |
| **Active** | Workers can receive tasks | Monitor submissions |
| **Paused** | Temporarily stopped | Resume or edit |
| **Completed** | Budget exhausted or ended | Clone or analyze |

### Managing Campaigns

- **Edit** — Change budget, instructions, or settings (only when paused)
- **Pause/Resume** — Stop and start campaigns as needed
- **Delete** — Remove draft campaigns permanently

## Reviewing Tasks

**URL:** `/advertiser/tasks/review`

When workers submit completed tasks, they appear in your review queue:

1. View each submission with the worker's proof
2. **Approve** — Accept quality work (wallet is charged)
3. **Reject** — Return with a reason (no charge)

> **Tip:** Review submissions promptly. Workers appreciate fast turnaround and are more likely to complete your future campaigns.

**URL:** `/advertiser/campaigns/health`

Monitor the health of your active campaigns:

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **90-100** | Excellent — campaign performing well |
| **70-89** | Good — minor optimizations available |
| **50-69** | Fair — consider adjustments |
| **Below 50** | Needs attention — review targeting or budget |

Factors affecting health:
- Completion rate vs. impressions
- Average approval rate
- Budget utilization
- Worker satisfaction

## Best Practices

### Budget Management

- **Start small** — Test with a minimal budget first
- **Monitor daily** — Check spending and completion rates
- **Scale winners** — Increase budget on campaigns that perform well
- **Pause underperformers** — Don't waste budget on ineffective campaigns

### Task Instructions

- **Be specific** — "Follow @taskbloom on Instagram" is better than "Follow our page"
- **Include URLs** — Make it easy for workers to find the right place
- **Set expectations** — Clarify what "proof" should look like

### Targeting Strategy

- **Start broad** — Target all countries initially to test demand
- **Narrow gradually** — Focus on regions with best completion rates
- **Consider time zones** — Campaigns get more completions during peak hours
