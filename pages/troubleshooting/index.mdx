# Help & Support

Find quick answers to common questions and issues.

---

## Account & Registration

### How do I create an account?

Go to `/register` and fill in your details. You'll need a valid voucher code (available from resellers or advertisers).

### I forgot my password

Click **"Forgot Password"** on the login page. Enter your email and follow the reset instructions sent to your inbox.

### Can I change my email?

Contact support at **support@taskbloom.co.uk** to request an email change.

### Why was my registration rejected?

- **"Email already registered"** — You already have an account
- **"Invalid voucher code"** — Check the code and try again
- **"All fields required"** — Make sure every field is filled in

## Tasks & Earnings

### Why can't I get a task?

| Possible Reason | What to Do |
|----------------|------------|
| Daily limit reached | Wait until midnight |
| Monthly limit reached | Wait until the 1st, or upgrade your package |
| Earning cap reached | Wait until the 1st, or upgrade your package |
| Task already in progress | Complete or cancel your current task |
| No package active | Purchase or renew at `/packages` |
| No tasks available | Check back in a few minutes |

### How long does a task take to review?

Typically **24-72 hours**. Some tasks may take longer depending on advertiser availability.

### Why was my task rejected?

Common rejection reasons:
- **Incomplete action** — The required action wasn't fully completed
- **Invalid proof** — Screenshot was unclear or didn't show the required result
- **Wrong account** — You used the wrong account or profile

You can try a different task after 24 hours.

### How do I cancel a task?

Go to the task page and click **"Cancel Task"**. You can only cancel tasks that are in "locked" or "in progress" status.

### My task expired — what happened?

Tasks have a **48-hour deadline**. If you don't submit within 48 hours, the task expires and you won't be paid.

## Wallet & Payouts

### What's the minimum payout?

The minimum withdrawal amount is **$10.00**.

### What payment methods are available?

You can withdraw via **PayPal, Stripe, Wise, or Crypto**.

### How long do payouts take?

Payouts are processed after a **72-hour hold period**. If it's been longer than 7 days, contact support.

### Why can't I request a payout?

- Balance below **$10.00** minimum
- Already have a pending payout request
- Account-related restrictions

### What are the payout fees?

A **2.9% + $0.30** fee applies to each payout transaction.

## Advertiser Questions

### Why won't my campaign launch?

- **Insufficient wallet balance** — Fund your wallet at `/advertiser/billing`
- **Invalid settings** — Check all campaign fields are complete
- **Already active** — You can only pause or edit active campaigns

### Why aren't tasks being completed?

- **Budget exhausted** — Check remaining budget
- **Targeting too narrow** — Broaden country/language targeting
- **Campaign paused** — Check campaign status

### How do I get a refund?

Unused campaign budget stays in your wallet and can be withdrawn anytime.

## Referral Program

### How do I find my referral code?

Go to `/referrals`. Your referral link is displayed on the page.

### When do I earn commissions?

Commissions are credited when your referral's task is **approved** by the advertiser.

### How many levels of commission are there?

TaskBloom offers a **3-level** referral system:
- **Level 1** (direct referrals): 5%
- **Level 2** (their referrals): 3%
- **Level 3** (their referrals): 1%

## Reseller Questions

### How do I purchase vouchers?

Go to `/reseller/orders/purchase`, select voucher type and quantity, then confirm.

### What's my profit margin?

Your profit depends on the wholesale price you pay vs. the price you sell to workers.

### How do I deposit funds?

Go to `/reseller/wallet` and click "Deposit Funds".
