## Quick Account Setup

### 1. Register

Go to `/register` and enter:
- **Email address**
- **Password** (minimum 8 characters)
- **Full name**
- **Voucher code** (required — get one from an advertiser or reseller)
- **Referral code** (optional — if someone invited you)
- **Country**

> **Tip:** Keep your password secure and use a unique one for TaskBloom.

### 2. Verify Your Account

After registration, you'll be logged in automatically and directed to your dashboard. No email verification required.

### 3. Explore Your Dashboard

Your dashboard is at `/tasks` (for workers) or `/advertiser/dashboard` (for advertisers).

## Understanding the Platform

### What Are Tasks?

Tasks are simple actions that advertisers pay you to complete. Examples:
- Following a social media account
- Watching a short video
- Leaving a Google review
- Completing a survey
- Downloading an app

### What Are Packages?

Packages determine your **limits** — how many tasks you can do per day and per month. Higher packages = more earning potential.

| Package | Price | Daily Tasks | Monthly Tasks |
|---------|-------|-------------|---------------|
| Bronze | $9.99 | 5 | 150 |
| Silver | $49.99 | 20 | 300 |
| Gold | $99.99 | 40 | 750 |
| Platinum | $499.99 | 100 | 1,800 |
| Diamond | $999.99 | Unlimited | Unlimited |

### What Are Vouchers?

Vouchers are digital codes that:
- Allow new users to register on the platform
- Enable package upgrades
- Can be purchased in bulk by resellers
